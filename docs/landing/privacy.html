KubePilot Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

KubePilot (“we”, “the app”) is an AI-assisted Kubernetes operations companion. This policy explains what the iOS app collects and how data is handled.

Who processes your data

The KubePilot iOS app connects to a KubePilot server that you configure (your own host or organisation). We do not operate a mandatory cloud backend for the mobile app. Data you load in the app is sent to the server URL you enter.

Data the app accesses

  • Account credentials you enter — server URL, username/password or token. Stored in the iOS Keychain on your device; used only to authenticate to your KubePilot server.
  • Cluster operations data — pod lists, events, logs, diagnostics, and AI troubleshooting results returned by your server.
  • Optional biometrics — Face ID / Touch ID only unlocks credentials already on device; biometric templates never leave the device.

Data we do not collect

  • No advertising SDKs or third-party analytics trackers are embedded in the iOS app.
  • No sale of personal data.
  • No Apple Advertising Identifier (IDFA).

On-device storage

The app may cache recent cluster summaries locally (SwiftData) to improve offline browsing. You can remove this data by signing out or deleting the app.

AI features

AI troubleshooting runs on the KubePilot server (and its configured LLM, such as a local Ollama instance). Prompts include operational context you request (for example pod logs). That processing is controlled by your server operator, not by a separate KubePilot consumer cloud.

Children

KubePilot is a developer / SRE tool and is not directed at children under 13.

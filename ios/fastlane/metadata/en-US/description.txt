KubePilot is an AI-assisted Kubernetes companion for SREs and platform engineers on the move.

Connect to your KubePilot server to see cluster health, problem pods, events, and logs — then run AI root-cause analysis when something is CrashLooping or failing.

Features:
• Live cluster health and problem-pod triage
• Pod logs, events, and diagnostics
• AI troubleshooting powered by your KubePilot backend
• Secure credential storage with optional Face ID
• Widgets for quick cluster status

KubePilot does not require a separate cloud account: you point the app at your own KubePilot server (for example on your LAN or in your organisation).
